About Me

I completed my doctoral research at IISER Kolkata, where I worked extensively on Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TDDFT) which now become workhorse across diverse areas of modern chemical research. During this period, I served as the lead developer of InDFT, an in-house electronic structure package designed for efficient atomistic and molecular simulations. For a detailed overview of my academic background, research experience, publications, and professional activities, please visit the CV section.

My postdoctoral experiences at two leading research institutes — TIFR Mumbai (India) and Northwestern University (USA) — have strengthened my appreciation to the fact that the strength of chemical sciences lies in the close interplay between theory and experiment, which provides an excellent platform for advancing new research directions. I am highly motivated to establish an independent research program that complements and strengthens the existing experimental and theoretical efforts at the institute, while actively fostering both national and international collaborations.

Areas of Specialization

1. Electronic Structure Theory

  • Development of new desnity functional approximations and efficient real-space implementations.
  • Development and application of advanced methods, including RPA, GKS-spRPA, single-shot GW, MR-CISD, and EOM-CCSD.
  • Development of ensemble density functional theory-based exciton model for challenging excited-state problems.
  • Periodic electrostatic embedded cluster models for the simulation and chracterisation of point defects in materials.

2. Non-Linear Electron Dynamics

Development of adiabatic subspace based real-time Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory and efficient real-space implementation.

3. Non-Radiative Processes

Development of singularity-free second-order nonradiative rate formalism that captures vibrational spin-orbit and spin-vibronic effects within a unified framework and enables quantitatively reliable calculations of reverse intersystem crossing (RISC) rates in thermally activated delayed fluorescence (TADF) materials.

4. Non-Hermitian Quantum Theory

  • Development of Complex desnity functional theory and efficient RPA implementations.
  • Development and application of advanced methods, including CBF-EOM-SF-CCSD, CAP-MR-CISD.
